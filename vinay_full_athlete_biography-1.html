About Vinay

Vinay is a young athlete from Bharu, Post Ojhaganj, District Basti, Uttar Pradesh. At the age of 17, he is building his journey in athletics with dedication, discipline and the dream of representing India on the international stage.

My Athletics Journey

2023 — The Beginning
Vinay's athletics journey began in 2023 at S.S. Sport Academy, Parivarpur, starting from a small field. What began with simple training gradually became a serious sporting journey.

State-Level Competition
With consistent training and hard work, Vinay has competed at the state level and has continued working toward higher levels of competition.

The Journey Ahead
Every competition and every training session is a step toward his bigger goal: representing India and winning a gold medal.
